My Experience

My learning journey using Figma and digital design.

devita

“Design is not just appearance, it is how everything works.”

Last week, I had a collaborative assignment that utilized Figma. I felt enthusiastic since it was my first time exploring the platform independently for a project.

However, the initial excitement quickly turned into frustration when I encountered a major technical issue.

Every time I attempted to rearrange my layout, I found it incredibly difficult to move the elements across the canvas.

Objects either remained completely frozen in place, or dragging them unexpectedly distorted the entire group of surrounding components.

To resolve this issue, I paused my work and began troubleshooting the interface settings.

I discovered that the root of the problem was twofold: some critical layers were accidentally locked in the sidebar, and I was unknowingly using the wrong selection tool.

I fixed the issue by pressing the V key to activate the standard Move Tool and systematically clicking the padlock icons to unlock my layers.

This experience taught me that mastering fundamental canvas navigation and layer management is essential before diving into any complex digital design project.
